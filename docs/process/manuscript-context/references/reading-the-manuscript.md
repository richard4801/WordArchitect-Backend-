# Reading the Manuscript

## How to Use Search Results

The project knowledge search returns chunks of the manuscript — fragments of chapters, sometimes mid-scene, sometimes across multiple chapters. The skill is reading them not for plot summary but for the specific things that inform the chapter you're about to write.

When a search returns results, look for:

**Physical and behavioral tells.**
The manuscript shows character through action and dialogue, not summary. When a search returns a scene, look at what the character *does*, not what they're described as feeling. Mina biting someone in a fight. Aldric pouring three glasses and handing them over. Sera punching Torin's face without thinking. These actions carry the character's truth more reliably than any description.

**What the character says when they're not trying.**
The off-guard moments. The sentence that slips out before the character can manage it. The thing they say to themselves that they wouldn't say to anyone else. These are where the character lives.

**What the character avoids.**
What they don't say in a conversation they're clearly affected by. What they change the subject away from. What they do with their hands while someone is talking to them. Avoidance is character.

**The specific texture of a relationship.**
How do these two characters stand relative to each other? Who controls the space? Who looks away first? What does one of them do that they only do with this specific person? These details are what make a scene between two characters feel like it's between these two people and not any two people.

## What the Manuscript Has Already Established — Don't Invent

The manuscript is 200+ chapters of established fact. Before inventing a detail — a character's reaction, a piece of their history, how they feel about another character — search to see whether the manuscript has already established it.

If the manuscript says Mina's mother died of wolfsbane when Mina was six, and Mina found her, that is not background texture to be deployed freely. That is the single most defining fact of Mina's psychology, and every chapter she appears in should be written with that fact actively in mind, even when — especially when — it isn't mentioned.

The things the manuscript has established are the bones. The chapter being written is flesh and muscle. The bones are not negotiable.

## Character-Specific Things to Always Pull

**Mina:**
- Half-sister to Fenris and Dimitri. Viktor's bastard by a Southern slave concubine.
- Mother died by wolfsbane when Mina was 6. The old Luna psychologically tortured her into it.
- Mina found her. She was running down the corridor and she already knew before she reached the door.
- Found out the full truth years later when the old Luna confessed on her deathbed. Mina's response: her mother still chose to leave. That was a choice.
- Dimitri was her only real protector growing up. He's dead.
- She and Sera bonded as two omegas trying to survive. Sera is the first person who ever looked at her and saw a person instead of a bastard.
- She is watching Sera use psychological destruction to break Irina and the echo of what the old Luna did to her mother is in every moment of it. She will not say this out loud. She may not even fully let herself think it.

**Sera:**
- Omega. Voluntary bet with her father, not forced arranged marriage. Lost the bet, was sent to Ironmaw.
- Was mated to Kane for three years. He took a second mate (Elara), who fell pregnant. The betrayal is still active, still shapes how she responds to being claimed.
- Now Luna of Ironmaw. Mated to Fenris.
- Has been making increasingly cold, precise decisions that get results and cost something she hasn't named yet.

**Fenris:**
- Alpha of Ironmaw. Dimitri's brother. Mina's half-brother.
- Not given to words. Communicates through action and silence.
- His relationship with Sera is the anchor of the manuscript.

**Aldric:**
- King of Valdris. Sera's father. Lyra's father.
- Warm, terrifying, always three steps ahead.
- Knows more than he shows. Acts on none of it openly.

**Irina:**
- Queen of Valdris. Sera's mother. Aldric's wife.
- Has been politically, personally, and sexually compromised during this visit to Ironmaw.
- Has just been broken by her own daughter.

## The Echo Question

Before writing any chapter, ask: **what from this character's past is echoing in this present moment?**

Not in a way that needs to be stated. Not in a way that the character necessarily recognizes. But in the way that the past always shapes the present — the reason a character flinches at something specific, reaches for something familiar, avoids looking at something directly.

The manuscript has already provided those echoes. The search finds them. The prose lets them operate underneath without announcing them.
