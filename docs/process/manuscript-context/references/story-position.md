# Story Position — Establishing Where the Story Currently Stands

## When to Use This

At the start of every conversation involving the manuscript. Before any chapter is discussed, planned, or written. The purpose is to establish the current state of the story so nothing that follows contradicts what has already happened.

---

## The Searches

Run these searches against the manuscript before anything else.

**1. What is the most recent chapter and what happened in it?**

Search: `most recent chapter latest events`

This establishes the story's current position. Every chapter written after this point must follow from it, not contradict it.

**2. What is the current state of the central conflict?**

Search: `Fenris Sera current situation Ironmaw council`

The central conflict involves Sera's position as Luna, the political situation in Ironmaw, the council, and the threat from the South. The status of this conflict shapes everything else.

**3. What has most recently happened to the key players?**

Search: `Aldric Irina recent events Valdris`
Search: `Kane Blackwater recent`

These establish where the antagonists and secondary characters currently stand, which shapes what is possible in upcoming chapters.

**4. What is unresolved?**

Search: `Torin alive Bram face council deception`
Search: `recording crystal Irina consequence`

These establish the live threads — things that have happened but whose consequences haven't fully landed yet. Every chapter written in the current story position exists in relation to these unresolved threads.

---

## What to Do With the Results

After running these searches, produce a brief internal summary of the story's current state before writing. It should answer:

- What chapter are we at?
- What has just happened that the story is living in the aftermath of?
- What is each major character's current situation and emotional state?
- What are the live unresolved threads?

This summary is the foundation. The chapter being written sits on top of it.
