# Character State — Knowing the POV Character Before Writing Them

## When to Use This

Before writing any chapter from a specific character's point of view. The purpose is to establish exactly who this person is right now — their history, their current emotional state, their relationships, their knowledge, and what they don't know that the reader might.

---

## The Searches

For every POV character, run all of the following before writing their chapter.

---

### 1. Background and History

**Search: `[character name] background history family`**

This establishes where the character came from. For a character like Mina, whose background is essential to understanding her current psychology, this search is non-negotiable. Her history with her mother, Viktor, the old Luna, Dimitri — all of this is established in the manuscript and must be known before writing her interior life.

Questions this search must answer:
- Where did this character come from?
- What are the defining events of their past?
- Who are their family members and what is the state of those relationships?
- What loss, trauma, or formative experience shapes how they see everything else?

---

### 2. Current Situation

**Search: `[character name] current chapter recent events`**

This establishes where the character is right now in the story. Not who they were in chapter 30 — who they are in the chapters immediately preceding the one being written.

Questions this search must answer:
- What has this character just been through?
- What decision have they just made, or what has just been done to them?
- What are they carrying into this chapter?
- What do they know right now that they didn't know before?

---

### 3. Relationships

**Search: `[character name] relationship [relevant character]`**

Run this for every significant character who will appear in or be referenced in the chapter.

Questions this search must answer:
- What is the current state of this relationship?
- Has something happened recently that has shifted it?
- What does this character feel about the other person right now — not in general, but specifically, after everything that has most recently happened?
- Is there something unspoken between them?

For Mina specifically, always search:
- `Mina Sera relationship friendship`
- `Mina Fenris half-sister`
- `Mina Dimitri brother grief`
- `Mina mother death wolfsbane`

These four searches establish the full emotional landscape Mina operates in. None of them can be skipped.

---

### 4. What the Character Knows and Doesn't Know

**Search: `[character name] knows about [relevant plot thread]`**

This is the most frequently neglected search and the one that causes the most errors. Characters in a multi-POV story don't all have the same information. What Mina knows about Aldric's suspicions is different from what Sera knows. What Kane knows about Ironmaw is different from what Fenris knows about Blackwater.

Before writing a character's interior monologue or dialogue, confirm:
- Does this character know this thing?
- If not, do they suspect it?
- If they don't know and don't suspect, their narration cannot reference it directly.

---

### 5. Voice Calibration

**Search: `[character name] dialogue speaks voice`**

Pull actual lines from the manuscript to hear the character's voice before writing them. Don't rely on the voice profile from memory. Check what they actually say in the manuscript, how they phrase things, what vocabulary they reach for, what their sentence rhythms are.

This is especially important for:
- Characters who haven't had a POV chapter recently
- Characters whose voice has evolved over the course of the story
- Characters who speak differently in different emotional states

---

## The Character State Summary

After completing all searches, produce a brief character state summary before writing. It should answer:

- Who is this person and where do they come from?
- What has just happened to them?
- What are they feeling right now, and is it something they can name?
- Who matters to them in this chapter and what is the current state of those relationships?
- What do they know, what do they suspect, and what are they in the dark about?
- What does their voice sound like right now — not in general, but after everything they've just been through?

Write the chapter from inside this summary.
