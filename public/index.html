Bulk import (whole manuscript, auto-split by chapter)

Paste an entire manuscript. Lines like "Chapter 1", "Chapter Two — Homecoming", or "CHAPTER 9:" are detected automatically and split into separate chapters. Runs as a resumable background job — one chapter is chunked and embedded per step, so even a very large manuscript processes safely without one long-lived request. Closing this tab pauses it; reopening offers to resume right where it left off.
